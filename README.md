# Orbi's Mission Lab

A browser game where kids and teens (1 to 4 players) design a real space mission with Orbi, a friendly robot co-pilot.
Made during the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 (14 and 15 November 2026) for the challenge "Space Mission Design Game".

Play: open `index.html` through GitHub Pages (Settings, Pages, deploy from the `main` branch, root folder).
This copy runs the solo game fully in the browser with no login and no server. Team rooms (2 to 4 players online) need the
server in the `server/` folder (Cloudflare Worker with Durable Objects).

## What you do
1. Pick a mission on the planet map: First Satellite, Fire Watch, Aral Sea Watch, Planet Hunter (space telescope), Moon Ice Finder, Red Planet Relay (Mars) or Jupiter Deep Dive.
2. Build a spacecraft from snap-on part cards and keep five meters green: weight, electricity, money, data and fuel.
3. Fly the launch yourself with real orbital physics, then brake into orbit at the Moon, Mars or Jupiter.
4. Survive emergencies with a timer: solar storms, space junk, tumbling (stop the spin by hand), budget cuts.
5. Get up to 3 stars, a score and a "Why this result?" explanation, plus a comparison with real missions.

Also: career mode (run your own space agency against a rival), Pilot School with medals and a Moon landing, a cabin window to photograph real places,
a photo album, a space quiz, space cards, a shop for Orbi, and three languages (English, Kazakh, Russian).

## Real science and data
- NASA FIRMS: MODIS Collection 6.1 near-real-time active fire detections, 7 to 14 November 2026 (Kazakhstan and Central Asia, Canada). Used in Fire Watch.
- NASA JPL Solar System Dynamics, "Approximate Positions of the Planets" (Keplerian elements): Earth and Mars positions for Mars launch windows.
- NASA reference missions for comparison: Landsat 9 (NASA and USGS), TESS, Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter, Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter, Juno, NASA CubeSat 101, NASA CubeSat Launch Initiative.
- Canadian Space Agency: WildFireSat mission goals. UNOOSA and JAXA: KiboCUBE.
- Physics in the game: the rocket equation, orbital mechanics, solar power and eclipse batteries, radio link budget, radiation dose, mission cost. Rocket prices are public 2025 and 2026 figures; some are estimates.

## Use of AI
The code and the game text were written during the hackathon with an AI assistant (Claude by Anthropic) on the Higgsfield website builder.
The Kazakh and Russian translations were also made with AI. All graphics are drawn by code (Orbi, planets, story pictures, cabin views,
the 3D spaceship with three.js, flying games). Music and sounds are generated by code in the browser.
This version contains no AI-generated images, video or audio.

## Files
- `index.html`, `client.js`: the game screens.
- `logic.js`: the game rules and the engineering model (shared by solo play and the server).
- `sim.js`: flight physics. `scene.js`: the 3D spaceship. `three.min.js`: three.js (MIT licence).
- `kk*.js`, `ru*.js`: Kazakh and Russian text.
- `server/`: the Cloudflare Worker for online team rooms and the test bots.
